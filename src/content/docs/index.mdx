## What it runs

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Media & photos" icon="star">
		A full media library and a private photo cloud for the family — streaming and phone backup. Your own Netflix + Google Photos, on hardware you own.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Docs & tools" icon="document">
		A searchable document archive, bookmarks, notes, a résumé builder, budgeting — a shelf of small self-hosted apps, each behind one login.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Private DNS" icon="setting">
		Network-wide ad & tracker blocking, split-horizon names so every service gets a real HTTPS address, and per-device rules — served locally.
	</Card>
	<Card title="One login, everywhere" icon="approve-check">
		Single sign-on across every app via a self-hosted identity provider. No per-app passwords; groups decide who sees what.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## The shape of it

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="One box" icon="laptop">
		A fanless mini PC with a stack of NVMe drives — all-flash, near-silent, a few watts of power. No rack, no separate NAS.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Proxmox + ZFS" icon="setting">
		A hypervisor base with mirrored ZFS storage: containers for isolation, snapshots for safety, and a clean line between hot data and backups.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reachable anywhere" icon="rocket">
		Private by default over a mesh VPN; selectively public through a tunnel — no ports forwarded, no home IP exposed.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Reproducible" icon="pencil">
		Reverse proxy, DNS, auth, and secrets are all configured as data you can rebuild from. These guides are those patterns, generalized.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Build your own

Every guide is written to be **copied, not admired** — the reasoning first, then the pattern, then the gotchas that cost real hours. Start with the [overview](/guides/overview/), or jump to whatever you're wiring up today.

:::note[These guides are generalized on purpose]
No real tokens, addresses, or topology from the live setup are published here — that would just be handing out an attack surface. You'll see the *patterns* and example configs with placeholders; swap in your own.
:::
